# The Red Book of Westmarch - Chapter III: Making It Visible

## Summary

In this assignment, we focused on making our selected system capability visible through a static interface. We designed and implemented how the system presents information and supports user interaction.
We also explained our design decisions, what we considered during implementation, and what we deliberately chose to leave out.

## AI Assistance

We used AI (ChatGPT) to support us in implementing the interface.

We described what we expected from our system and how we wanted the interface to look and behave. Based on this, the AI generated code which we then reviewed, adjusted, and refined to match our intentions.

We also used AI to help us formulate and structure our thoughts more clearly, especially when describing our design decisions and system behavior.

We noticed:

- faster translation of ideas into code
- support in structuring HTML and CSS
- improved clarity in articulating our ideas

We ensured that all outputs were reviewed and aligned with our original concept.

## Lessons Learned

We learned that:

- translating ideas into a concrete interface requires precise thinking
- even simple interfaces need clear structure and visual hierarchy
- design decisions strongly influence how easily users understand the system
- reviewing and adjusting AI-generated code is essential to maintain control over the result
