# Artifact 3 TFC: Represantion

## Selected System Capability

The selected capability is the Safe Route Decision System. It supports the Fellowship in choosing the safest and most efficient path towards their destination.

We chose this capability because decision-making under uncertainty is a critical challenge during the journey. The Fellowship must constantly balance speed, safety, and available resources.

At this stage of the journey, the risk of encountering enemies such as Nazgûl or orcs is high. Therefore, having structured support for route decisions is essential for survival and mission success.

## Design Rationale

**i. How does this interface support the intent and value defined in Assignment 1?**

The interface supports the intent by helping the Fellowship make informed decisions under uncertainty. By clearly presenting alternative routes, highlighting risks through icons, and structuring information in an easily digestible way, it reduces confusion and enables more confident decision-making. This directly contributes to the value of increased safety, better resource planning, and reduced conflict within the group.

**ii. How does it reflect the wireframe from Assignment 2?**

The implemented interface closely follows the structure of the wireframe, especially in terms of layout and hierarchy. The separation of the “Route Options” heading, the clear grouping of route elements, and the placement of the decision button reflect the planned structure. Visual refinements such as rounded containers and icons were added to improve clarity while staying aligned with the original concept.

**iii. What did you deliberately not implement?**

Interactive behavior and dynamic system responses were not implemented. The interface remains static, meaning that route selection, risk updates, or real-time changes are not functional. Additionally, deeper system features such as live data updates or complex navigation logic were intentionally left out to maintain focus on clarity and structure.

**iv. What assumptions or constraints shaped your decisions?**

Several constraints influenced the design. The Fellowship operates under uncertainty, with incomplete and potentially outdated information, which required the interface to emphasize clarity and quick comprehension. The users (hobbits) are assumed to be inexperienced and easily overwhelmed, so the design avoids complexity. Furthermore, technical limitations (static implementation) and the possibility of limited communication shaped decisions such as simple navigation structures and clear visual guidance across the interface.
